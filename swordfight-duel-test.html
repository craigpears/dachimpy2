Puzzle Pirates' Swordfight, two boards, both dealt the same pairs. Break a breaker on its colour and what breaks goes at the other side: loose pieces as sprinkles, a block fused 2x2 or bigger as a sword, a chain multiplying it. It waits over their board and comes in when their pair lands, coloured by your sword's drop pattern: a sword picture for a turn, then grey, then see-through, then ordinary. Fill their fourth column and they lose; a sword landing there is an insta. Left and right arrows move, up and down turn, Space drops.
