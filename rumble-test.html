Rumble

Brawl a bot Watch two bots

Aim with the mouse over your board (or drag on it, on a phone; or the arrow keys). Both fists aim together. Fire the left fist with Z or the left button, the right with X or the right button (on a phone, the two buttons below). Balls bounce off the sides.

Three of a colour clear, and anything left hanging falls. That sends sprinkles: a row of 9 comes up from the foot of their board once you've made enough.

Hold a fire button about a second to charge the ball: a group it makes stays, charged, numbered in order. Hit the first one with an ordinary ball and every group charged after it goes too: a combo. Two groups or more is a strike, pushed in at the top corner of their board (your left fist hits their right). Loose balls in a combo turn some of the strike into bruises, dark balls that join no group until two clears beside them.

An attack lands after their next shot. Every eighth shot a new row comes in at the top. Balls below the line, and you're out.
