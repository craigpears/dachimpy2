How it's designed Play the den

12 November

A bribe, two gambles, and a chart of the race

The whole sloop race on one sheet of old parchment, drawn from the course itself: the long way round its five numbered buoys, and every shortcut through the spits, with the puzzle it takes and the seconds it saves. The snakes are in red, the winds in blue and the gambles in gold. Hatched green are the sheltered spots, where even the leader can stop and puzzle without being shot. Beside each way round is how often it wins, from thousands of races sailed by bots: every way wins between about one race in five and one in three, against a fair one in four. So pick the shortcuts that are your puzzles.

A bribe at the gate. The gun gap on the first spit is an imperial gate now, with a gatehouse, the Empire's banners and a guard who can be bought. Back along the first leg an old wreck lies aground in a sheltered notch: haul up 40 coins by Treasure Haul, hand them over, and his guns hold their fire while his men tow you through. Or rush it on a big combo, or shoot its guns quiet, as before.

Two gambles, a bet not a puzzle. On the second leg a whirlpool turns its spin back and forth on a beat you can watch: sail in as it's spinning your way and it flings you on through a cleft to the next leg; mistime it and it throws you back to the first. Through the last spit a veiled wind shimmers, but there's no telling which way it blows until you're in it: half the time on to the run home, half the time back down the leg. Neither is worth more than sailing round on average, so they're best kept for when you're behind.

More on the thinking behind it in How the Pirate Den is designed.

Watch the race

Sit back and watch four racers go round with nobody else in it: Kikkit, Tzz, Dalnoth with Umami aboard, and Gravee, who takes the locks. Fly the camera anywhere over the course, or press 1 to 4 to follow a racer, Tab for the next. A bar along the top says where she's placed, which leg she's on and what she's up to.

Follow one and you see her puzzling, move by move: her Sailing board as she sails and builds her Vegas, Gravee's Bilge on the lock's quay, Umami's Foraging on the driftwood, Dalnoth loading his guns, and a patch on her deck when she's hit.

Try it: den.html?at=race-watch

11 November

The sloop race

Out past Primates Rule, race Kikkit, Tzz and Dalnoth round a zigzag course: pick your spot on the starting line past a field of whirlpools and winds, then five legs back and forth round numbered buoys to a proper finish line. Every bend has a long way round that needs no puzzle, and a shortcut through the spit that needs one: a gap guarded by guns (a heavy combo zooms you past, or knock them out by Gunnery), a headwind you row through, whirlpools only a Vegas clears, two locks pumped by Bilge, a crane you build by Carpentry, and a barrier of driftwood cleared by Foraging. Each one saves about what it takes to play, so take the ones that are your puzzles.

Each rival has her own sloop, colours and way round: Kikkit goes all out for a Vegas over the whirlpools, Tzz hops the little gaps with Doubles and never fires a shot, and Dalnoth takes the driftwood with Umami the capybara aboard to forage. Sit still out in the open and they'll shoot you, and you can shoot back with Q and E; a hit leaves a sloop smoking until it's patched. The locks, the crane's ledge and the barrier are sheltered. Watch for the snakes, trade winds that sweep you a long way back (a well-timed Double hops them). Your place shows as you race, everyone's places and times at the finish, and your best time's kept. The course was tuned over thousands of simulated races so every route has a fair chance.

Duty Navigation

Puzzle Pirates' Duty Navigation, drawn as the real thing: an upright chart of the night sky, three rings to turn, stars dropped in to fall towards the middle, lines of three to clear, and constellations to fill before you booch.

Try it: navigation-test.html

Port Decorum, the imperial island

Out past the imperial checkpoint, the empire's own port: a fort whose walls tower over your sloop's mast, its keep the tallest thing on the sea, two-storey town buildings along the quay, and a jetty to tie up at. A first sketch of a place to sail to.

Try it: den.html?at=imperial

Tzz's balloon lesson

A hop from Kikkit's island, Tzz blows up balloons and plays them onto a real Sailing board beside her, showing you how a chain's really built, step by step: the gap left as the trigger, the stack of three, the balloon that drops into the gap, all marked on the board. Then it's your go.

Try it: den.html?at=tzz

Gravee's house: Bedside Manors

Knock at the houses in the den's cliff. The first is Gravee's. She walks you round her portraits and talks about each one you look at, the vampire sits up in his coffin, and she paints you scared to death for her wall.

Try it: den.html?at=lodgings, then Gravee's house

Kikkit's whirlpools

Kikkit on her little island beside the reef, explaining whirlpools (taken literally) by Puzzle Pirates' rules, with plenty of tangents. Sail through the reef by them, and with a Vegas reach the penguins on Vegas Island beyond.

Try it: den.html?at=kikkit

Mischievious's wedding

Sail out to the Wedding Isle, where Mischievious is always planning the next one. Lay out the venue, dress the guests, pick who you marry and how it goes, and keep a collage of the day. Whoever you marry sails home with you and lives at the den.

Try it: den.html?at=wedding

Smaller updates

Primates Rule

Dachimpy's war frigate, built to YPPedia's numbers: two rows of guns, a quarterdeck, a figurehead and a crew of skeletons. Seen beside your sloop for scale, and anchored out at sea, coming soon.

Dalnoth and his Vikings

The raiders at your wall are Vikings now, horned helmets and all, and Dalnoth is their chief. He shakes his fist from his ship's deck before he rows in.

10 November

Umami's rescue

A crane you build by Carpentry hauls the sloop up the cove's cliff into a still, reedy lagoon. On its far bank Umami the capybara is shut in an old fish trap. Free him by Foraging and he comes home to live in the den's shallows.

Try it: den.html?at=umami

The imperial checkpoint, and Dignity

Its guns are trained on marks in the water, so getting past takes some work. You can run it, row it, knock its guns out, or find the secret way round by the locks. The first time you sail up, its commander Dignity peers down from the gate, and her monkey Bella fires the warning shot.

Try it: den.html?at=dignity

Rowing

Row the sloop by port and starboard: two boards of mirror strokes, one for each side's oars. The turn you're building shows on her: the leading side's oars sweep harder, and her bows swing and heel toward it (the compass in this first shot has since gone). It's the way through calms and headwinds, and through the checkpoint's guns.

Try it: den.html?at=sea, then the oars

9 November

The captain's map

Beat the opening siege and Dalnoth calls it off in a short, cheesy cutscene: "We'll be back!" A map falls out of his coat as he runs for his boat. It shows the sea as it really is, with an X on an old wreck.

Try it: den.html?at=map-scene

Out to sea in the sloop

Once the siege is over, an old sloop is left at anchor in the cove. Board her and sail on Battle Navigation's moves, with a gun each side loaded by Gunnery on her deck. Trade winds sweep you out to sea.

Try it: den.html?at=sea

Bilging the shallows, and powder barrels

The real Bilge board lies on the beach, and its water is the tide. Good moves draw the tide back so bare rocks cut off the Vikings rowing in.

Clear two lines in one swap and you dredge up a powder barrel. It lands on the sand by the wall and blows any Viking who comes by flat.

Try it: den.html?at=built, then out through the gate to the beach

8 November

Swordfight at the armoury hoist, and the soldiers

Beaten Vikings drop crates of weapons. Carry them to the armoury and play them on its hoist by Swordfight's rules.

A broken chest of swords arms little soldiers in blue and white, who go and hold the breaches. Loose crates are broken up into planks that mend the wall.

Try it: den.html?at=start, then take the dropped crates to the hoist

7 November

Your shack

The shack's plot is marked on the den floor from the start. Build it whenever you like, wall by wall and then the roof, every part a Carpentry frame filled piece by piece.

Try it: den.html?at=shack

Dress your pirate

A wardrobe of Puzzle Pirates clothes, dyes and familiars, plus your own flag and den. Since 10 November the real pirates are dressed from the same wardrobe, so you can wear anything they wear.

Try it: den.html, then "Your pirate"

The gun tower

The top of the gun tower is a Gunnery board. Chalk arrows steer the stores into the guns, and the loaded guns fire on the raiders. The same Gunnery is later played on the sloop's deck.

Try it: den.html?at=gun-tower

Building by Carpentry

Everything in the den is built from pieces that stay pieces: the walk along the wall, the stairs and the gun tower are laid out as frames on the floor and filled a piece at a time, then go up.

Since 9 November there's also the Carpentry view: Tab takes you straight to the wall or any frame, with Puzzle Pirates' own keys.

Try it: den.html?at=walk

The den, mended against raiders

Where it began: the den's wall is broken and raiders are coming. Grab pieces from the crates and mend the holes by Carpentry. Fill a hole exactly for a Masterpiece.

Try it: den.html?at=start
