Oars against the water

Pairs come in from the top as the water's flow. A round breaker pops every piece of its colour it touches, and what falls after can pop more (a chain). The flow presses each oar back a notch a pair: that's the drive, firmer the more of its colours lie on the board. Right back, the oar's spent. A pop swings it forward, further the bigger the pop (a chain counts double, triple...). Red and amber pull port, teal and blue starboard: both oars together keep her straight, one alone turns her. Reach the top and the water washes the top rows away.
