Distilling positions

Moments from the practice players' own games, frozen: whites low by the fire, a white walled in near it, a practice player waiting with something stuck, a column about to go up with junk in it, the block phase, the fire's side early on. Each shows the practice player's move there now. Say what it should do instead, in chat, and it becomes a test the practice players have to pass from then on. New ones, still to judge, come first; the ones you've judged follow, by theme.
