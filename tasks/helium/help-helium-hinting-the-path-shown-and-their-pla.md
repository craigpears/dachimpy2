"as well as helium helping, they can be hinting as well, so it shows you the path. Of what the next moves are they would do. And maybe ... plans out a few of like, well, this is the thing I will do at this part of the game." So a hint mode beside the help: the next drag or two drawn on the board as a path (not played), and what they're working towards at this stage in a line (to shape with the user). And with Helium's nerves (the same day): "when he's kind of highlighting the white pieces ... maybe it should show some arrows of nearby routes of pieces that can be dragged into the spots": with the whites ringed, arrows for the nearby junk pieces' drags that would take their places (the path search's routes). And three ways at the still (the same day): Help (they play it), hints and feedback, or "the watch helium play option where he explains, well ... first of all ... I'm going to try and get rid of all the white pieces in all these columns, and then just kind of step by step what you're thinking based on the whatever technique or strategy". So the bots need a phase of their own they say aloud ("this is the current phase I think I'm in"), and the phase sets each technique's weight: "it can always use all the techniques if it absolutely needs to, like it gets stuck or there's a blocked white piece. But ... different techniques have different weights depending on the stage." Done 13 Nov: the bots' phases (fire, fill, block, chain) with weights by phase; hints on G (arrows, and routes into a ringed white); watch Helium on W. Then ("you've got 2 of the 3 in that column, but there's no route shown for how that third white can get out. It should show all of them"): every white in the columns a hint clears gets a pale route of its own, and every white by the fire one before any gets two.
