**Asked:** 21 Nov: "the hard distilling four furnaces thing, which I actually do think deserves its own page, it should also have a what it is tab and also the thinking behind it ... for high-level distillers, hitting crystal clear 12 is relatively easy. There's nothing that differentiates you. So this, the idea here is to bring in more tactics. Do you go fast or do you go slow to be efficient ... Do you go for a double crystal clear to maximize your spice? ... moving pieces speeds up the burn. It's actually impossible to just get perfect runs of crystal clears is the intent. Subject to balancing ... do I go and burn lots of junk at the start? Do I do one crystal clear at a time, double together to get more spice? Also, negative points on spice burn just create more pressure to act instead of just waiting around and building up a long crystal clear chain in a similar mechanical way each game."
