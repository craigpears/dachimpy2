looking at Helium's portrait (black and white, the Atlantis trinket, a dark purple monkey on the shoulder): "I don't feel like the character really does her any justice ... I can see she's been given the ears, but like the whole tone and colour of everything, like the shading on the [portrait], she looks really good, but her character in the game really does not. To be honest, I think I found the same with [Tzz] as well. I think the quality of their appearance really needs working on." So: a pass on how the real pirates look in 3D, starting with Helium and Tzz, matched against their portraits: colours and tones, shading, proportions, their pieces (the cat-eared mask, the Atlantis trinket in Helium's hand, left out before as it's not in the wardrobe; the monkey's dark purple). One phrase was lost to dictation ("Why should she be given a poke?"; perhaps the hair, drawn as a bun): to pin down with the user. Then (14 Nov), of Sanne's portrait in Dachimpy's museum: "looking less cringy, but again, I feel like the quality can keep going up. The same things we did with [Tzz] and Helium, that we just look like a couple of Playmobil figures in that. I want it to be a bit higher quality than Playmobil." Then, correcting that: "I do like the [Playmobil] style thing to an extent, but just not to the extreme that it is. Like, I like how blocky people are for a large part, but I think things like hair and clothing need more ... there was a change we made to [Tzz] and Helium to improve the quality of their hair, among other things. I just want the same. It feels like that kind of thing has not been applied to these same styles." So the blocky bodies stay; the hair and the clothes get what Tzz's and Helium's got on 13 Nov (hair built of strands and locks with a streaked texture, garments cut and detailed from the portrait), applied to the other hairstyles (long, ponytail, bun, braids, wavy, curly, short, the beehive) and clothes, starting with the ones the museum's friends wear.
