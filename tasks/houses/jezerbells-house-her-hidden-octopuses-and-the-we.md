## Log

- 21 Nov: added.
- 21 Nov: Who (from the 21 Nov chat): Jezerbell (she; a real pirate, spelt Jezerbell in game). Runs werewolf dens and vampire lairs, beginner ones so new crew get a shot; "she eats WW for breakfast" (Edpit), took three wolves alone. Her gallery is her main house; her shack is her bank ("my shack has my poe in it"). Hides her octopus familiars so nobody asks for them. Wanted the wine bat. Helium’s lesson: "no your other left jez", so they did karaoke instead. Keep it gentle: leave out who outbid her for the bat, and her "blonde question" joke about Tzz, unless she’s happy with them.
- 21 Nov: Her house, from the screenshot (21 Nov): two summoning altars at its heart, the werewolf stump with pink-eyed skulls in its roots ("Summon werewolves") and a gothic altar ("Summon vampires"); a yellow throne with bat wings; bat gargoyles on chests; two doubloon exchanges; a gold suit of armour, a blue-and-white vase, a potted palm, a shark statue, a sea turtle and a seal by the stairs; portraits "Twilight Stroll", "Eternal Love!" and "Just the three of us" (ask her about them); route charts along the wall ("Gauntlet Island to Barbary Island"...). Her gallery and shack could come later.
- 21 Nov: The hunt for her octopuses: hidden round the house (peeking out of vases, frames, the throne), found one at a time, her pleased you noticed and a bit nervous you’ll ask for one. One is missing: the werewolves have it, so summoning them is how you get it back.
- 21 Nov: The fray, as Puzzle Pirates has it: a werewolf charm (used up) at the summoning stump starts a fray in the house, 5 pirates against the pack, each fighting by Swordfight or Rumble; win, and everyone gets some coins and one winner a Wolf-skull Wayfinder. The Wayfinder lasts 3 days; on the chart table of a cutter or smaller it starts a werewolf expedition, a battle with the werewolf ship at sea, then the den on Woodhallow. Sources: yppedia.puzzlepirates.com/Werewolf_expedition and /Wolf-skull_wayfinder; the summoning furniture seen in the screenshot (YPPedia only had the vampire one).
- 21 Nov: In the den: her house the front door to Multibella’s wolf den, joined by one item, the Wayfinder (her house, then the expedition at sea, then the wolf den, each its own island). The fray first by Swordfight, which the den has; Rumble once its basics card is done. Lintmint turned up to the real one dressed as a werewolf, in orange: see Lintmint’s card for the joint adventure.
