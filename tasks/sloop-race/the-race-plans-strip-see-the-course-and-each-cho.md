sitting on the race's planning: "if you're changing the, you need to be able to see the length. This is very disconnected. At the moment ... it requires you to really know the course to actually be able to do anything with this." The strip lists each leg's choices by name only. So show the course with your route drawn on it, each choice's line on it as you change one, and how long each takes against the long way, so a plan can be made without knowing the course. And (13 Nov): "persist the last plan you chose so that you don't need to do it every time." **Done 13 Nov, to be played:** the strip has the course drawn small with your route in gold and where she is; each leg's choices side by side, each with its seconds against the long way (from the simulation's measures: src/race-sim.ts, choiceTime; tested); pointing at one draws its line on the course and says it, a click or tap takes it; on a phone a tab opening as a sheet. Your last plan's kept in this browser and each race starts from it (src/race-plan.ts, readPlan; tested). RACE.md §5.
