*Sunk at the checkpoint: row home in a little boat (10 Nov)*

Take too many hits running the checkpoint and the sloop goes down; you bob up in a little rowboat and row home, which is where many players first learn to row.

> "Would it be funny if she actually destroyed your ship and you then had to row back to shore in a little rowboat, doing the rowing puzzle ... that's how you discover that you can ... learn how to row." (10 Nov)
> "not things you just casually get through on the first try ... it should be work." (10 Nov)

- **A scene, not a game over:** Bella's last shot, Dignity's giggle, then the rowboat.
- **Rowing is how you get home:** mirror strokes; once they can row, the winds guarding the wreck and the canal are theirs to beat (the secret still never said).
- **Not a punishment:** a short row, the sloop back at the den (mended, or a new one); the cost is time and pride, never progress.
- **The checkpoint stays possible:** a bold run with a hit or two can still get through; sinking is for pushing your luck.
- **Open:** how many hits sink her; whether the rowboat starts at the checkpoint or partway home.
- **Rowboats, and expeditions (15 Nov):** "that still comes up ... I've just not figured out how to make it work": little rowboats as a thing of their own, the latest example launching them from the ships in a blockade, which is how you actually go and capture points.
