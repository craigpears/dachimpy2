*Moments you can play: emotes, and painting the scene (14 Nov)*

"Maybe actually we need like an animation that you can play, like, you know, blowing up a balloon, giving some
cookies to somebody, getting your pet familiar to go fire some guns." So the little moments from the cutscenes
become emotes your own pirate can play anywhere: blowing up a balloon animal (Tzz's), offering cookies round
(Dachimpy's), your familiar off to fire the guns (Balloffire's juggling act would fit here too). The cutscene is
then the same moment done properly, a higher quality version, and the emote the quick one you play yourself.
And "any point you can stop and draw a picture of the scene that you're at, like with Graves style": pause
anywhere and have the scene painted as a picture, the way Gravee's easel paints your portrait. (The user said
"disguised scene" and "emojis": read here as "the cutscene" and "emotes", to pin down with the user.)
