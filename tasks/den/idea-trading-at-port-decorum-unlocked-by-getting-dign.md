*Trading at Port Decorum, unlocked by getting Dignity on side (14 Nov, vague, for the list)*

After being turned away at Port Decorum's jetty on Helium's rum run: "maybe ... a good quest for later ... you
unlock trading ... I don't know if Dignity could be involved in that. Like, you know, that somehow you get her on
side and it gives you also the legitimacy so that the guy at the fort doesn't immediately flag you as a pirate.
Don't really know how that works. That's just kind of a vague idea for the list at the moment." Not necessarily
Dignity: "for somebody or some kind of line like that." A quest that wins you standing with the Empire, after which
the jetty guard lets you trade. With a doubt about trading itself: "I find the trading very awkward because ... it's
not very meaningful when it's single player and ... the game design is designed to be quick and fun rather than
deep in the sense of ... persistent." So whatever it unlocks should be quick and fun, a scene or a keepsake, not an
economy.

- **Gravee taking portraits of things (14 Nov):** out of the race ("better as a side character. I don't feel like
  she's suited to the race part"): "I want her later to be taking portraits of things. That fits her very well."

- **Playing as Umami, a reward for rescuing him (14 Nov):** "being able to place [play] the capybara would be a great
  reward for rescuing him." Today anyone can (the wardrobe's Capybara, den.html?as=umami); as a reward, the
  wardrobe's Capybara would open once he's freed and aboard, said so when he hops off at the den.

- **The whirlpools' Vegas stored for later (14 Nov):** "the Vegas one ... not only is it not that much longer,
  that's like just a really hard shortcut ... unless you really set it up in advance. Like, I feel like the only way
  that makes sense is if you could kind of save the Vegas for later ... you take the long way around and you can
  store and charge up the Vegas and then that lets you past ... So it's more like you can use that shortcut if you
  have done a Vegas at any point. That would make more sense maybe than sitting there waiting. Otherwise, I can
  never see that being worth it." (RACE.md, the whirlpools' shortcut; a change to its rules, to design.)
