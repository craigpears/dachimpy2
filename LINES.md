## Dalnoth

Captain of the raiders who lay siege to the den, and the one at their ship's cannon from its first shot. A real Puzzle Pirates pirate, Dalnoth of Emerald (the user's friend), drawn from his portrait with affection: a Viking helmet with yellow fur, a big black beard, a long gold coat, a frothy tankard of ale in his hand and his white monkey on his shoulder. A bit of a drunk: the ale slops as he rants, and he's unsteady on his feet.

Beaten after the third wave, he leaves his cannon, rows in, stomps up the beach and shakes his fist at your wall, then runs for it, and his map falls out of his coat. To come: guarding his treasure on his island, his white monkey hopping onto your shoulder when you beat him, and back, furious.

## You

Your own pirate, dressed as you like from the wardrobe (or as any real pirate in it). You say little, and only at the den's short, cheesy moments: picking up the captain's map, and riding the crane up the cliff and down again.

### Picking up the captain's map, at the end of Dalnoth's cutscene

- "A map!"

### Hauling her up the cliff by the crane, or back down (over your head)

- *Leaping up to the windlass, to haul her up into the lagoon:* "Up she comes!"
- *Leaping up to the windlass, to lower her back down into the cove:* "Down she goes!"
- *Winding her up out of the water:* "Heave!"
- *Winding her the rest of the way up:* "Ho!"
- *The jib swinging her round:* "Easy... easy..."
- *Lowering her in:* "Mind her paint!"
- *Leaping down onto her deck:* "Wheee!"
- *There: up in the lagoon:* "Ooh, a lagoon!"
- *There: back down in the cove:* "Home sweet cove"

## The den's pirate voice

Not someone you meet: the den's own voice, a pirate's ("yer", "ye", "o'"), in the captions of the little tour round the captain's map. It only says what's there to see and how to work a thing: never how to get past the checkpoint, nor where a secret is (RULES.md, "Hidden gems, never hinted").

### The tour round the captain's map, the first time you open it (T shows it again)

- *At your den and the sloop:* "Yer den, and the old sloop they left ye, at anchor off the beach. Walk out to the end o' the jetty and press Space to board her."
- *At the trade winds and the shipping lane:* "The trade winds sweep her out from the beach, and the shipping lane runs on out to sea. Click the sea to drop a pin and she sails there herself, or steer her with W, A and D."
- *At the imperial checkpoint:* "The Imperial checkpoint. Pirates not welcome, and its guns say so. There's more than one way past it, mind. Which? That's for ye to find."
- *At the old wreck and the X:* "Beyond it, the open sea, and an old wreck aground on a sandbar. X marks the spot: the captain's treasure, there for the hauling."
- *At your quests:* "And that's only what the captain drew. There's more off the edges o' this map. Yer quests are on the scrap by it, ticked off as ye go: the den's jobs, and what's still out there to find. T shows ye this again."

## Dignity

The imperial checkpoint's dutiful commander, peering down from the top of its gatehouse, the Empire's flag behind her. A real Puzzle Pirates pirate (Dignity of Emerald), drawn from her portrait: smiling, wavy blonde hair, an eyepatch, a dusky purple Buccaneer jacket, a brass spyglass in her hand, and Bella, her lavender monkey, on her shoulder.

Witty, very cute, and a stickler for the rules (she hates paperwork, and writes very cross reports). Pirates are always a disappointment to her. She sends Bella to fire the warning shot, and giggles at it.

### Her cutscene, the first time you sail up to her checkpoint

- *Lowering her spyglass: she's seen you:* "Halt! Imperial checkpoint. State your busi..."
- *A hand to her forehead:* "...oh. Pirates. It's always pirates."
- *To Bella, pointing her spyglass at you:* "Look lively! And do try to hit them this time."
- *The warning shot just missed you: a hand to her mouth, then wagging her spyglass at you:* "Hee hee! Oops. That was a warning shot. Mostly. Come any closer and the next ones won't be."

### Later, over her head as things happen (each said once)

- *You come one stroke short of her guns' first mark:* "One more stroke and I'll have to shoot. There'll be paperwork. I hate paperwork."
- *You wreck one of her guns:* "That was Imperial property!"
- *You get through her gate, either way:* "This is going in my report. In very small, very cross handwriting."
- *You come out round her by the locks (even if she never saw you sail up):* "Nobody uses the canal. Nobody!"

## Bella

Dignity's lavender monkey, on her shoulder (a limited-edition monkey colour: what looked like little purple wings in the portrait is her curled tail). She's the one who fires the warning shot: she chatters, scampers down in quick monkey bounds to one of the checkpoint's real guns, salutes very stiffly, lights the fuse, covers her ears, and strikes a proud little pose, a fist pumped in the air.

> "The lavender monkey has got to be involved somehow ... the little monkey is the one that fires the cannon at you." (the user, 10 Nov)
>
> "We've got to get these familiars really involved" (the user, 10 Nov)

### Dignity's cutscene

- *Sent to the gun: bouncing on Dignity's shoulder, arms waving:* "Eek eek!"
- *Her proud little pose on the gun, after the shot:* "Eek!"

## Umami

A capybara, the den's first rescued friend (a friend's side quest: the friend asked for the name). Shut in an old driftwood fish trap on the far bank of the clifftop lagoon, and never hinted at beforehand: you find him. Freed by Foraging, he bursts out of his trap, bounds along the bank three times for joy, swims out to her and clambers aboard onto her foredeck. Then he lives at the den, in the shallows by her mooring: wading, swimming, sitting at the water's edge.

He doesn't speak (yet): his lines are what's said of him.

> "a pet that you found and rescued" (the user, 10 Nov)
>
> "being in the cage or being trapped in some way visually makes sense while you're trying to free them. They break free and happily start jumping around" (the user, 10 Nov)

### His rescue

- *Poling her along the lagoon to his bank, the first time:* "A capybara, shut in an old fish trap on the bank!"
- *Off the foot of his Foraging board: his trap bursts open:* "He's free! Umami the capybara"
- *He's clambered aboard onto her foredeck:* "Umami's aboard!"
