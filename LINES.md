## Dalnoth

Captain of the Vikings who lay siege to the den, and the one at their ship's cannon from its first shot. A real Puzzle Pirates pirate, Dalnoth of Emerald (the user's friend), drawn from his portrait with affection: a Viking helmet with yellow fur, a big black beard, a long gold coat, a frothy tankard of ale in his hand and his white monkey on his shoulder. A bit of a drunk: the ale slops as he rants, and he's unsteady on his feet.

Beaten after the third wave, he leaves his cannon, rows in, stomps up the beach and shakes his fist at your wall, then runs for it, and his map falls out of his coat. To come: guarding his treasure on his island, his white monkey hopping onto your shoulder when you beat him, and back, furious.

## The den's pirate voice

Not someone you meet: the den's own voice, a pirate's ("yer", "ye", "o'"), in the captions of the little tour round the captain's map. It only says what's there to see and how to work a thing: never how to get past the checkpoint, nor where a secret is (RULES.md, "Hidden gems, never hinted").

### The tour round the captain's map, the first time you open it (T shows it again)

- *At your den and the sloop:* "Yer den, and the old sloop they left ye, at anchor off the beach. Walk out to the end o' the jetty and press Space to board her."
- *At the trade winds and the shipping lane:* "The trade winds sweep her out from the beach, and the shipping lane runs on out to sea. Click the sea to drop a pin and she sails there herself, or steer her with W, A and D."
- *At the imperial checkpoint:* "The Imperial checkpoint. Pirates not welcome, and its guns say so. There's more than one way past it, mind. Which? That's for ye to find."
- *At the old wreck and the X:* "Beyond it, the open sea, and an old wreck aground on a sandbar. X marks the spot: the captain's treasure, there for the hauling."
- *At your quests:* "And that's only what the captain drew. There's more off the edges o' this map. Yer quests are on the scrap by it, ticked off as ye go: the den's jobs, and what's still out there to find. T shows ye this again."

## Dignity

The imperial checkpoint's dutiful commander, peering down from the top of its gatehouse, the Empire's flag behind her. A real Puzzle Pirates pirate (Dignity of Emerald), drawn from her portrait: smiling, wavy blonde hair, an eyepatch, a dusky purple Buccaneer jacket, a brass spyglass in her hand, and Bella, her lavender monkey, on her shoulder.

Witty, very cute, and a stickler for the rules (she hates paperwork, and writes very cross reports). Pirates are always a disappointment to her. She sends Bella to fire the warning shot, and giggles at it.

## Umami

A capybara, the den's first rescued friend (a friend's side quest: the friend asked for the name). Shut in an old driftwood fish trap on the far bank of the clifftop lagoon, and never hinted at beforehand: you find him. Freed by Foraging, he bursts out of his trap, bounds along the bank three times for joy, swims out to her and clambers aboard onto her foredeck. Then he lives at the den, in the shallows by her mooring: wading, swimming, sitting at the water's edge.

He doesn't speak (yet): his lines are what's said of him.

## Mischievious

Host of the Wedding Isle, out at sea on the den's side of the checkpoint: she runs the wedding venue on her island and is always planning the next one. A real Puzzle Pirates pirate (Mischievious of Emerald, with the extra i), drawn from five of her portraits, and you choose which of her you'd marry, each by its own title: "Son of a Nutcracker!", "What is Love?", "Taco Princess", "Wicked" and "Hotter Than Hell".

Giggly, gushing, delighted with whatever you do: never a rule you can fail. Funny about herself, never mean about herself (the dress already on, "a dress that's hardly been bled on"). Cheeky, never dirty: a wink in a line about the furniture is the right amount. She's the host, not the show: her chatter is the wedding's and the Ocean's (the tailor, dyes and doubloons, the inn's grog, the furnisher, security earning their pay), not her own love life. She's been through six ceremonies today for the fun of it, each one of the wedding's own options, so her stories show you what you can choose.

In Happy and cheesy she's all giggles and romance; in Dark humour, not in the least bothered by the blood and guts of the last wedding, with a bit of extra security hired and the mop in the corner.

Marry her, and she comes home with you: aboard the sloop, then at the den.
