Tabletop blockade

Puzzle Pirates' blockade, as it plays. Each turn, put a move in each of its four slots (a 3-move ship rests one) and the guns you'll fire off each side, then Sail: everyone moves at once, slot by slot; winds push you a square, whirlpools carry you round; then the guns. A buoy only your side's ships reach scores its pennants every turn; a sloop has to sit on it, a frigate holds the most. A slot: left click a left (again to go round), middle click a forward, right click a right; a gun's circle: click it on, right-click it off. Keys: A W D a move in the next slot, Q E a gun off the left or right in the last slot you set, X clears, Space sails.
