Duty Navigation

Puzzle Pirates' Duty Navigation, by its real rules and keys. Up and Down choose a ring (or point at one), Left and Right turn it (or click: left anticlockwise, right clockwise), Space drops the star waiting on its spoke. Three of a colour in a line, out from the middle or round a ring, vanish. Fill the constellation's places with their colours. Don't drop a star onto a star in the outer ring.
