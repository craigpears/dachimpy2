Distilling

Puzzle Pirates' Distilling, by its real rules and keys. Click a piece, then a neighbour, to swap them (or hold and drag it through swaps). The lower piece must beat the upper: white rises past black, brown past white, black past brown. Arrows or the number pad move the cursor, Space swaps, X or right-click burns the column now. A column with more blacks than whites burns; the rest go up into the flask. Only whites: Crystal clear!
