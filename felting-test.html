Felting

Inspired by Lintmint, who said i like sheep wool stabbing and cheej :3, so here it is: needle felting as a puzzle.

A tuft of wool on a foam pad, the pattern's outline faint on it. Tap or click to stab the barbed needle in.

1. Tuck in the strays. Stab the stray wool outside the outline and it's pulled a cell in toward the shape.

2. Firm it evenly. A stab firms its cell, and the cells beside it a little. Firm is the goal; stab a spot too often and it goes hard, a dimple. The middle firms from all sides, so the edges need more stabs of their own. Tucking wool in softens where it lands, so tuck first.

3. The details. Pick a colour, tap to lay a wisp of it, then tap again to stab it in (twice and it's in). Those stabs are shallow but firm the wool under it a little, so leave those cells a touch soft. A cheese's holes are dimples on purpose: stab them hard.

Three needles. Stab too fast, or drag while you stab, and the needle bends; do it again while it's bent and it snaps. Wait a moment and it straightens. Finish when you're happy: it's scored on the pattern, how even the felt is, and needles left.
