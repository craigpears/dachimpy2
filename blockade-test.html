Blockade

Hold the buoys: each turn, a buoy only one side's ships reach scores its pennants. The best are in the fort in the middle: sloops ride the winds in by the side ways; a war frigate has to shoot a tower of an imperial gate down. Sloops hide in the islands' coves and dart out. Each side's war brig fights round the islands, launching her craft: Tidecaller's turtles ram, Grave Lantern's ghost sloops hold buoys a while. Click a ship below to follow her, or Sail to take her helm: click the sea to sail, W A D moves, Q E broadsides, X stops.
